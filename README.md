NONSMANONLINE QUALITY GADGETS & ACCESSORIES INVENTORY ANALYSIS DASHBOARD

  PROJECT REVIEW
An inventory analysis project for Nonsmanonline Quality Gadgets & Accessories, focused on product availability, pricing, inventory value, and price distribution.

  TOOLS USED
- Excel – Data cleaning
- SQL – Data analysis and validation
- Power BI – Dashboard development and visualization

    PROJECT PROCESS
1. Cleaned the raw data using Excel.
2. Exported the cleaned data to SQL.
3. Used Excel and SQL to analyze and validate the findings.
4. Used Power BI to create the interactive dashboard.
5. Developed business insights and recommendations from the findings.

    KEY INSIGHTS
- 422 total products were recorded.
- 267 products were available, while 155 were unavailable.
- Total inventory value was ₦12,412,550.
- Average product price was ₦29,414.
- Highest product price was ₦726,900.
- Lowest product price was ₦1,600.
- 393 products fall within the ₦0–₦99,999 price range.

    INSIGHTS & RECOMMNEDATIONS
- The majority of products fall within the ₦0–₦99,999 price range, showing a strong concentration of lower-priced products. The business should maintain a balanced product range across different price categories.
- 155 products are currently unavailable. The business should monitor stock availability and identify products that require restocking.
- The inventory has a total value of ₦12.4M. Regular inventory monitoring can help the business manage its stock value effectively.
- High-value products such as Seagate One Touch should be monitored for demand and profitability.

    DASHBOARD
The Power BI dashboard provides an overview of inventory levels, product pricing, expensive and cheapest products, and price distribution.

    CONCLUSION
The analysis provides useful insights into product availability, pricing, and inventory distribution, helping the business make better inventory management decisions.
